Our Mission

At Aurora Adult Primary Care, our mission is to deliver accessible, compassionate, high-quality primary care through both in-office and home-based services. We strive to remove barriers to healthcare by offering timely visits, personalized care plans, and flexible access to care. By focusing on preventive care, chronic disease management, and continuity of care, we empower adults and seniors to live healthier, safer, and more independent lives — while restoring dignity, trust, and meaningful connection to the patient–provider relationship.

Our Team

Meet Your Provider

Irina Danilova, MSN, APRN

Founder & Primary Care Provider

Irina is a board-certified adult geriatric primary care nurse practitioner with a deep clinical focus on healthy aging, disease prevention, and personalized primary care for adults navigating midlife and beyond. She works with patients who want more than rushed visits and reactive medicine — individuals who seek to understand why their health is changing and how to maintain strength, sharpness, and independence as they age.

Irina has a strong understanding of the biological and hormonal changes that occur during midlife, including metabolic shifts, cardiovascular risk, cognitive changes, and menopause-related transitions. She is a member of The Menopause Society (formerly NAMS) and stays current with evidence-based guidance for menopause and perimenopause management.

With years of experience in adult and geriatric care, Irina takes time to listen, educate, and partner with her patients. She believes aging is not something to fear or ignore — it is a process that can be managed thoughtfully with the right medical guidance, lifestyle strategies, and preventive care.

At Aurora Adult Primary Care, Irina offers longer, unhurried visits and a relationship-based model of care. Patients are treated as individuals — not diagnoses, not numbers, and not insurance claims. Her mission is simple: to help adults feel informed, supported, and confident in their health decisions — today and as they age.

Membership Plans

Experience Direct Primary Care

A membership model designed to provide comprehensive, affordable, and accessible healthcare tailored to your needs — without constraints of insurance.

✅ Why Choose DPC?

  • Personalized, unhurried visits with the same provider
  • Transparent, affordable pricing with no hidden fees
  • Direct access to your provider when you need it
  • DPC membership available to ages 21–64 (non-Medicare recipients)
  • Patients 65+ can receive services under Medicare Part B if qualified

📋 What You Must Know

  • DPC does NOT replace insurance
  • DPC requires a monthly subscription
  • You may use your HSA account (depending on your insurance carrier) to pay for membership

📦 What We Offer Through DPC

  • Adult Primary Care & Preventive Services
  • Hormone Evaluation & Replacement Therapy
  • Peri- & Post-Menopause Care
  • Chronic Disease Management
  • Weight Management & Metabolic Health

Direct Primary Care or DPC is an innovative alternative payment model where the provider charges the patient an affordable monthly subscription fee. The DPC allows patients to gain unrestricted access to their provider and address health needs that are not typically covered by insurance, such as weight loss medications, bioidentical hormone replacement treatments, functional medicine, etc. The amount of the fee usually depends on the patient's needs, chronic conditions, and age. DPC does not replace insurance plans that may pay for certain laboratory testing and imaging, regular medications, or specialty/emergent care.
