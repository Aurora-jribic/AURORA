About Us

Our Mission & Values

Built on compassion, clinical excellence, and a commitment to making healthcare accessible to every adult.

Our Mission

At Aurora Adult Primary Care, our mission is to deliver accessible, compassionate, high-quality primary care through both in-office and home-based services. We strive to remove barriers to healthcare by offering timely visits, personalized care plans, and flexible access to care. By focusing on preventive care, chronic disease management, and continuity of care, we empower adults and seniors to live healthier, safer, and more independent lives — while restoring dignity, trust, and meaningful connection to the patient–provider relationship.

❤️ Compassionate Care

We treat every patient with kindness, respect, and genuine compassion. Our goal is to build trusting relationships and provide care that honors each individual's dignity, preferences, and quality of life.

🏡 Home-Based Primary Care

Healthcare should be accessible to those who need it most. We bring comprehensive primary care directly to patients in private homes, independent living, and assisted living communities, reducing the burden of travel while delivering high-quality medical care.

🩺 Clinical Excellence

We are committed to evidence-based medicine, thoughtful clinical decision-making, and individualized treatment plans that address each patient's unique health needs and goals.

🤝 Partnership & Care Coordination

Exceptional care is a team effort. We work closely with patients, families, caregivers, specialists, home health agencies, therapists, and facility staff to ensure seamless communication and coordinated care.

🛡️ Dignity & Independence

Our mission is to help patients maintain their health, independence, and quality of life while receiving medical care in the comfort and familiarity of their own living environment.

❤️ Prevention & Continuity

We focus on preventing avoidable hospitalizations, managing chronic conditions, and providing consistent, ongoing care that supports long-term health and well-being.

📚 Education & Shared Decision-Making

We believe informed patients and families make better healthcare decisions. We take the time to explain diagnoses, treatment options, and care plans so every patient can participate confidently in their healthcare journey.

Meet Your Provider

Irina Danilova, MSN, AGPCNP

Founder & Primary Care Provider

Irina is a board-certified Adult-Gerontology Primary Care Nurse Practitioner dedicated to providing compassionate, personalized medical care for older adults. With more than 20 years of healthcare experience, she is passionate about helping Medicare patients maintain their health, independence, and quality of life.

She provides comprehensive home-based primary care for patients in private homes, independent living, and assisted living communities. Her approach combines evidence-based medicine with thoughtful listening, individualized treatment plans, and close collaboration with families, caregivers, specialists, and home health agencies.

Irina believes every patient deserves the time, attention, and respect needed to make informed healthcare decisions. Her mission is to deliver high-quality medical care where patients are most comfortable, helping them remain healthier, safer, and more independent.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services, insurance, and pricing.

Medicare no longer requires patients to have a medical reason for not going to the office; it only requires a medically necessary reason to be seen by a healthcare provider, which makes most seniors with Medicare eligible for house calls.

We proudly accept Original Medicare Part B and provide home-based primary care for eligible Medicare beneficiaries.

We also accept select Medicare Advantage PPO plans that include out-of-network benefits. Coverage and patient responsibility vary by plan, and benefits will be verified before your first appointment.

We do not participate with Medicare HMO plans, including Humana Medicare Advantage plans.

If you're unsure whether your plan is accepted, we're happy to verify your coverage before scheduling your visit.

We do not accept Medicaid.

We draw labs in the office only for an additional fee of $20. In-home, we may refer you to a mobile lab that comes to your house and collects necessary lab specimens if you desire. However, depending on the lab, this service may cost you an additional convenience fee.

We refer our patients to a pain specialist in your area for management of chronic pain.

We do not administer IV fluids or medications but refer to IV clinics whenever such treatment is prescribed and medically necessary.
